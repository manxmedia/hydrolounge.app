Iron /blood boost

When is an Iron Infusion Needed?

Iron infusions are recommended in the following situations:

  1. Severe Iron Deficiency Anemia: When hemoglobin levels are critically low.
  2. Inability to Tolerate Oral Iron, Some people experience side effects like nausea, constipation, or stomach pain from oral iron supplements.
  3. Poor Iron Absorption,Conditions like celiac disease, Crohn's disease, or gastric bypass surgery can impair iron absorption.
  4. Chronic Blood Loss Heavy menstrual bleeding, gastrointestinal bleeding, or other conditions causing ongoing blood loss.
  5. Pregnancy: If oral iron is insufficient or not tolerated during pregnancy.
  6. Kidney Disease Patients with chronic kidney disease (especially those on dialysis) often require iron infusions.
